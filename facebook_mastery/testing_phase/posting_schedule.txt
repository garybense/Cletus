FACEBOOK PERSUASIVE MESSAGE TESTING - POSTING SCHEDULE

OVERVIEW:
A/B testing schedule for 3 persuasive message templates over 2 weeks
Each template will be tested with variations to optimize engagement
All posts will follow ethical guidelines and compliance requirements

WEEK 1: INITIAL TESTING (Days 1-7)
Day 1 (Monday): Template A1 - Value-Driven Approach (Morning 9-11 AM EST)
Day 2 (Tuesday): Template B1 - Storytelling Approach (Afternoon 1-3 PM EST)
Day 3 (Wednesday): Template C1 - Question-Based Engagement (Evening 6-8 PM EST)
Day 4 (Thursday): Template A2 - Value-Driven Variation (Morning 9-11 AM EST)
Day 5 (Friday): Template B2 - Storytelling Variation (Afternoon 1-3 PM EST)
Day 6 (Saturday): Template C2 - Question-Based Variation (Evening 6-8 PM EST)
Day 7 (Sunday): Rest day - analyze preliminary data

WEEK 2: OPTIMIZATION TESTING (Days 8-14)
Day 8 (Monday): Best performing template from Week 1 (Morning 9-11 AM EST)
Day 9 (Tuesday): Second best performing template (Afternoon 1-3 PM EST)
Day 10 (Wednesday): Third template or new variation (Evening 6-8 PM EST)
Day 11 (Monday): A/B test timing variation - Best template at different time
Day 12 (Tuesday): A/B test content variation - Best template with minor tweak
Day 13 (Wednesday): A/B test CTA variation - Best template with different CTA
Day 14 (Sunday): Final analysis and reporting

VARIATIONS TO TEST:
- Timing: Morning (9-11 AM), Afternoon (1-3 PM), Evening (6-8 PM) EST
- Content: Minor tweaks to hooks, benefits, or CTAs
- Format: Text only vs. text with relevant image (if applicable)
- CTA: Different low-pressure options (comment "INFO", react, DM, etc.)

ENGAGEMENT METRICS TO TRACK:
- Reach/Impressions
- Likes/reactions
- Comments
- Shares
- Click-throughs (if linking to resource)
- Message inquiries (if using DM CTA)
- Conversion to next step (if applicable)

POSTING GUIDELINES:
- Space posts at least 4 hours apart to avoid audience fatigue
- Monitor and respond to comments within 2 hours during business hours
- Never use automated responses that violate Facebook's policies
- Ensure all content complies with Facebook's advertising policies
- Disclose any affiliations or sponsored content clearly
