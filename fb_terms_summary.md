# Facebook Automated Data Collection Terms Summary (Effective October 7, 2024)

## Key Provisions

### 1. Prohibition on Unauthorized Automated Data Collection
- You will not engage in Automated Data Collection without first obtaining Meta’s express written permission or in any manner that is not explicitly authorized by Meta. (Section 2)

### 2. Definition of Automated Data Collection
- "Automated Data Collection" refers to the collection of data from Meta Company Products via the use of automated or programmatic tools capable of navigating or indexing the surface-layer of the World Wide Web. These tools may include but are not limited to web scrapers, bots, robots, spiders, crawlers, user-agents, and other automated or programmatic mechanisms designed to access, collect, or retrieve data or content directly from web pages or applications. (Section 8)

### 3. Pathway to Express Written Permission
- Meta’s express written permission to engage in Automated Data Collection is subject to your agreement to abide by these Terms, which incorporate by reference the Meta Terms and Policies. For avoidance of doubt, acceptance of these Terms alone does not constitute the required written permission to conduct Automated Data Collection; such permission must be obtained separately through Meta’s formal authorization process. (Section 3)

### 4. Permitted Uses of Collected Data
- The Collected Data shall only be Used for the purpose of (i) providing results for your Search Engine, or (ii) displaying previews of Meta URLs to your users, and (iii) for other purposes for which you have received Meta’s express written permission to Use Collected Data. All other Uses are prohibited. (Section 4)

### 5. Restrictions on Personal Data Collection
- You will make no attempt to perform Automated Data Collection of Personal Data that is not Publicly Available Personal Data.
- You will comply with the Data Protection Addendum terms available at https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/Privacy (or a successor location). (Section 4)

### 6. Compliance Review and Technical Requirements
- You agree that you will ensure that you are in compliance with these Terms, and that Meta may review your Privacy and Security Program and your Use of Collected Data from time to time, at Meta’s sole discretion.
- You will comply with Meta’s robots.txt protocols, page header tags, and similar opt-out protocols that Meta implements.
- You will only use IP addresses, user-agent strings, and other identifiers that identify your services. (Section 4 & 5)

### 7. Retention and Deletion Requirements
- Period for which Personal Data will be retained shall be until your permitted Automated Data Collection and legally valid Use of such Publicly Available Personal Data has concluded, after such time you will promptly delete all such Publicly Available Personal Data.
- You will keep and maintain complete and accurate books and records in connection with your Use of Collected Data for a period of no less than one (1) year following the expiration or termination of these Terms. (Section 4 & 5)

### 8. Enforcement and Termination
- Meta reserves the right to implement measures to restrict your Automated Data Collection and Use of Collected Data at any time for any reason.
- Meta may take enforcement action including revoking your permission to engage in Automated Data Collection, requiring you to stop Using Collected Data, requiring you to delete Collected Data, and terminating other agreements. (Section 3 & 5)
