🎯 Objective: Build connection and trust through narrative
📊 A/B Test Variable: Personal story vs. general advice framing
⏰ Recommended Posting Time: 12-2 PM on weekends (leisure browsing)

📝 TEMPLATE:
Want to share a quick story from last week that reminded me of something important about [topic]...

It started when [brief relatable situation - e.g., "I was struggling with X", "A friend asked me for advice on Y", "I noticed a pattern in my daily routine"].

At first, I [initial reaction/action - e.g., "tried the usual approach", "felt frustrated", "wasn't sure where to start"].

Then I realized [key insight/turning point] - and that's when things shifted because:

✨ [Lesson 1 discovered]
✨ [Lesson 2 discovered] 
✨ [Lesson 3 discovered]

What surprised me most was [unexpected outcome or realization]. It's not about [common misconception] but rather [core truth/principle].

🔒 COMPLIANCE NOTE:
- Authentic personal narrative (not fabricated)
- Invitational, not prescriptive language
- No automated collection of personal stories shared in comments
- Clear that responses are voluntary and public
- Respects privacy - no pressure to share personal details

📈 TRACKING METRICS:
- Story completion rate (reading to end via time-on-post if available)
- Comment sharing of similar experiences
- Engagement depth (thread replies, not just surface reactions)
- Emotional tone analysis of responses
- Cross-post comparison with non-narrative content
