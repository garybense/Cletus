Subject: From [Before State] to [After State] - Here's How [First Name]

Body:
Hey [First Name],

BEFORE: Picture yourself dealing with [specific frustrating situation] every day.
You feel [emotion 1], [emotion 2], and wonder if things will ever change.
You've tried [common solutions] but they only provide temporary relief.

AFTER: Imagine waking up feeling [positive emotion 1], [positive emotion 2], and [positive emotion 3].
Your [specific area of improvement] is transformed, giving you [specific benefit].
You finally have [desired outcome] that lasts.

BRIDGE: That transformation is possible with our [Product/Service].
Here's exactly how it works:
1. [Step 1] - addresses [root cause]
2. [Step 2] - builds [positive habit/result]
3. [Step 3] - ensures [long-term sustainability]

What makes this different:
• [Differentiator 1] - [brief explanation]
• [Differentiator 2] - [brief explanation] 
• [Differentiator 3] - [brief explanation]

Real results: [Specific metric] improvement in [timeframe] for [percentage]% of users.

Your turn to experience the change: [Call-to-Action Button/Link]
[Special offer/gurantee] - because we're confident in the transformation we deliver.

Ethical Notes:
- Before scenarios are realistic, not exaggerated
- After scenarios are achievable and truthful
- Bridge mechanism is clearly explained
- No false promises or guaranteed specific results
- Clear disclosure of what's required for success
