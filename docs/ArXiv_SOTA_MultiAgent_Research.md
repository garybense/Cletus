#### 1. [A hyperbolic area proof of the Kahn-Lyubich Covering Lemma and its generalization](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17326v1)
- **Authors**: Kostiantyn Drach
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:59:23Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17326v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > The Covering Lemma of J. Kahn and M. Lyubich is a fundamental analytic tool for controlling conformal moduli of annuli under branched coverings of finite degree. In the last several decades, it has played a crucial role in establishing rigidity of multicritical polynomial and rational maps. The classical proof uses the Near-Degenerate Regime as the main mechanism. In this short note, we prove this lemma using hyperbolic area and the Dirichlet energy. We also establish a version of the Covering Lemma for holomorphic maps of infinite degree.   Our argument is inspired by recent applications of the hyperbolic area method to the study of wandering domains in rational and transcendental dynamics.

#### 2. [AgentSSL: Can MLE Agents Leverage Unlabeled Data?](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17323v1)
- **Authors**: Ethan Lin, Akanksha Sarkar, Ziang Liu
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:59:06Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17323v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > Applying machine learning systems to new domains introduces bottlenecks in both labeled data acquisition and manual pipeline engineering. While recent MLE agents show promise in automating pipeline design, they have been studied almost exclusively in fully supervised settings, leaving them untested in realistic regimes where labeled data is scarce but unlabeled data is abundant. In this work, we introduce Agentic Semi-Supervised Learning (AgentSSL), framing SSL as a program search problem and studying how agents navigate this space under limited supervision. We find that even under weak feedback and matched compute, agents can synthesize code that combines and adapts established SSL techniques, exceeding the strongest human-engineered baselines in 8 of 12 settings by up to +21.8%. However, our results identify program selection as a critical bottleneck in AgentSSL, where performance drops by as much as 47% when selecting based on the agent's feedback metric. To systematically study this bottleneck, we introduce ProgramSelectBench, a large-scale benchmark with a combined total of 16,977 agent-generated and human-engineered SSL programs, and evaluate both unsupervised and active program selection methods. We further evaluate AgentSSL on real-world ecology detection tasks, highlighting the opportunities and challenges of transferring agent-discovered strategies beyond controlled benchmarks.

#### 3. [Ensemble phase transitions in measurement-unitary hybrid quantum circuits](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17322v1)
- **Authors**: Saptarshi Mandal, Sthitadhi Roy
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:59:03Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17322v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > The universal properties of state ensembles generated by quantum dynamics provide a refined lens into Hilbert-space ergodicity and deep thermalisation, or lack thereof. We investigate deep-ergodicity breaking transitions of an ancillary qubit entangled with a system undergoing dynamics via hybrid quantum circuits, where unitary evolution is interspersed with projective measurements. Specifically, we analyse the statistical properties of two distinct state ensembles: the trajectory ensemble (TE) conditioned on the measurement outcomes of the hybrid circuit, and the projected ensemble (PE) generated by further projective measurements of the system at the final temporal boundary. We find that both ensembles undergo non-analytic changes -- ensemble phase transitions -- at the critical point of the measurement-induced purification transition, despite their first moments remaining completely maximally mixed and blind to the transition. For Haar-random circuits, the TE transitions from a trivial identity ensemble to the uniform Haar ensemble, while the PE transitions from the Haar ensemble to a trivial singleton pure state. In Clifford circuits, absence of non-stabiliserness limits the PE to a $1$-design, while the TE forms a $k$-design for $k \leq 3$. We demonstrate that these genuine ensemble transitions offer a clear physical mechanism for the measurement-induced entanglement or purification transitions, illustrating how the capacity to ergodically steer a quantum subsystem shifts from the temporal boundary measurements in the mixed phase to the bulk trajectory measurements in the pure phase.

#### 4. [The Cauchy problem for skew-torsion-parallel spinors with applications to supergravity](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17319v1)
- **Authors**: C. S. Shahbazi
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:58:38Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17319v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > We solve the Cauchy problem for an irreducible real spinor parallel with respect to a metric connection with skew-symmetric torsion on a globally hyperbolic Lorentzian four-manifold, proving that it is well-posed modulo an obstruction that we identify explicitly, and obtaining an initial-data characterization of such spinors. Our proof relies on the theory of spinorial forms, which we use to explicitly integrate the coupled flow for the spinor and underlying metric via a rotated pull-back action of the flow generated by a vector field on the Cauchy hypersurface $Σ$. We characterize the corresponding constraint equations as an explicit exterior differential system and describe all its solutions in terms of contact structures and flat foliations, obtaining a complete classification in the compact case. In particular, we conclude that every oriented compact three-manifold arises as the Cauchy hypersurface of a globally hyperbolic Lorentzian four-manifold equipped with a skew-torsion parallel spinor. Building on these results, we study the interaction between the four-dimensional Neveu-Schwarz supergravity evolution flow on an abelian bundle gerbe and the spinorial evolution flow determined by its supersymmetric conditions, which we prove to be well-posed and compatible. This establishes the well-posedness of the supersymmetric Neveu-Schwarz system in four dimensions and provides an initial-data characterization of supersymmetry. Furthermore, we obtain a strong rigidity result stating that every globally hyperbolic supersymmetric Neveu-Schwarz solution with a compact Cauchy hypersurface is flat and has vanishing bundle-gerbe curvature.

#### 5. [Countable dynamical systems with a dense orbit](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17315v1)
- **Authors**: Jhon Pérez-Remolina, Carlos Uzcátegui-Aylwin
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:58:20Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17315v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > We study countable dynamical systems with a dense orbit. Given an infinite compact metric countable space $X$, we show that $\mathcal{DO}(X)$, the collection of continuous functions on $X$ to itself admitting a dense orbit, is always nonempty. We then classify the systems in $\mathcal{DO}(X)$ up to topological conjugacy in terms of the second Cantor--Bendixson derivative $X^{(2)}$: if $X^{(2)} = \emptyset$ then all systems in $\mathcal{DO}(X)$ are mutually conjugate, while if $X^{(2)} \neq \emptyset$ then $\mathcal{DO}(X)$ contains $2^{\aleph_0}$ pairwise nonconjugate systems. The proof of the latter relies on a gluing construction for countable dynamical systems with a dense orbit, which is of independent interest. We also show that $\mathcal{DO}(X)$ is nowhere dense in $C(X,X)$ with the compact-open topology.   We also study the Ellis semigroup $E(X,f)$ of a countable dynamical system. We characterize, under the assumption that $|ω(x,f)| = 1$ for each $x \in X^{(1)}$, when the nontrivial functions of $E(X,f)$ are continuous. In particular, for the family constructed on spaces with $|X^{(2)}| \geq 2$, every nontrivial function of the Ellis semigroup is discontinuous.

#### 6. [Quantum Metrology and Quantum Battery in a Hyperfine-Coupled Spin System](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17310v1)
- **Authors**: Omar Bachain, Mohamed Amazioug, Rachid Ahl Laamara
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:56:57Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17310v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > We investigate multiparameter quantum metrology and the energetic properties of an effective electron--proton spin system inspired by the hyperfine structure of the hydrogen atom. Considering the external magnetic field $\mathcal B$ and the hyperfine coupling strength $Λ$ as unknown parameters, we derive the quantum Fisher information matrix and establish the corresponding quantum Cramér--Rao bounds for their simultaneous estimation. We analyze the estimation precision and the correlations between the two parameters, showing that the weak-commutativity condition is satisfied despite the noncommutativity of the associated symmetric logarithmic derivatives. Independently, we explore the energetic properties of the system under a unitary charging protocol, characterizing its ergotropy, anti-ergotropy, storage capacity, and energy-basis coherence. We investigate the dependence of these quantities on the magnetic field, hyperfine coupling, temperature, and charging time. Our results provide a comprehensive characterization of the metrological properties and energetic performance of a hyperfine spin-based quantum system, highlighting its potential as a platform for quantum sensing and energy storage.

#### 7. [Statistics of Fock-space correlation lengths in the many-body localised phase](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17306v1)
- **Authors**: Bibek Saha, David E. Logan, Sthitadhi Roy
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:56:50Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17306v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > The presence of long-ranged, many-body resonances in the many-body localised phase of disordered quantum systems is manifest in strong inhomogeneities of eigenstate amplitudes on the Fock space. We develop a quantitative, microscopic theory for this, centred on two-point Fock-space correlation functions. Exact diagonalisation of the disordered tilted-field Ising chain uncovers a stark contrast: typical correlations decay perfectly exponentially, whereas average correlations exhibit subexponential decay with systematic system-size dependence driven by rare resonant nodes. We show that this behaviour stems from an underlying distribution of Fock-space correlation lengths with a variance that scales inversely with system size. Crucially, the interplay of this distribution with the high-dimensional geometry of the hypercubic Fock-space graph, leads to the qualitative difference between the typical and average behaviours. The theory also allows us to derive the singularity spectrum of the many-body localised eigenstates, and to establish theoretically the anomalous symmetry violation of quantum multifractality as an inherent consequence of the Fock-space geometry.

#### 8. [Where LLM Agents Get Stuck Debugging Databases: Access More Than Reasoning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17304v1)
- **Authors**: Gustavo Pinto
- **Published Date**: 2026-11-12T18:56:22Z
- **arXiv Link**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17304v1
- **Abstract & Key Insights**:
  > Large language model (LLM) coding agents are increasingly asked not only to write code but to debug running systems from live runtime data. We report a field observation of one such agent (Claude Code) as it diagnosed eight database incidents in a production message-dispatch engine. In every incident whose root cause depended on runtime concurrency, the agent announced a cause, shipped a fix, and later walked it back, and each wrong guess cost a deploy-and-observe cycle. Mapping each incident to what the agent lacked, we find that access to production state was the most pervasive bottleneck. The missing access included live lock views, untruncated deadlock reports, read-only execution, and an explicit environment context. An access gap was present in all five concurrency-dependent incidents: alone in one, and in four combined with a reasoning error such as anchoring on an early cause, incomplete exploration of the configuration, or a misreading of the code's transaction semantics. The three incidents that did not depend on concurrency needed nothing beyond what the agent already had. Within the incidents, the agent's wrong hypotheses were consistent with the evidence it could see; they were not fabrications. We argue that the next step for production-debugging agents is a forensics layer that gives the agent the access it lacks inside existing security controls. Such a layer would combine a safe interrogation API, a lock-and-deadlock flight recorder, and a production replay harness.
