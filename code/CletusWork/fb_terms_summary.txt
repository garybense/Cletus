FACEBOOK AUTOMATED DATA COLLECTION TERMS - KEY POINTS SUMMARY
Effective October 7, 2024

PERMISSIBLE AUTOMATED DATA COLLECTION:
1. Requires Meta's express written permission (acceptance of Terms alone is NOT sufficient)
2. Collected Data may ONLY be used for:
   a) Providing results for your Search Engine
   b) Displaying previews of Meta URLs to your users
   c) Other purposes ONLY with Meta's express written permission
3. Prohibited uses include:
   - Transferring, selling, licensing or sublicensing Collected Data to any third party
   - Licensing of search indices and caches
   - Commercial search services providing substantially unlimited queries using derived data

REQUIREMENTS FOR PERMISSIBLE COLLECTION:
4. Must establish and maintain a Privacy and Security Program to safeguard Collected Data
5. Must implement technical mitigations (rate limiting, monitoring, etc.) to protect integrity/security
6. Must comply with Meta's robots.txt, page header tags, and similar opt-out protocols
7. Must only use IP addresses, user-agent strings, and identifiers that identify your services
8. For Personal Data: May ONLY collect Publicly Available Personal Data (not restricted by privacy settings)
9. Must comply with Data Protection Addendum (DPA) where you act as a "Controller"
10. Must retain Collected Data only until permitted use concludes, then promptly delete

COMPLIANCE & ENFORCEMENT:
11. Meta may review, monitor, and audit your Privacy and Security Program and Use of Collected Data
12. Must keep complete and accurate books/records for at least 1 year after expiration/termination
13. Must provide certifications/attestations of compliance when requested by Meta
14. Meta may suspend/terminate permission for violations, negative impact, legal compliance, or liability protection
15. You must indemnify Meta for damages/losses related to your Use of Collected Data

DEFINITIONS:
- "Automated Data Collection": Collection via automated/programmatic tools (scrapers, bots, crawlers, etc.) capable of navigating/indexing surface-layer of WWW
- "Publicly Available Personal Data": Personal Data made available to general public without privacy restrictions in Meta Company Products
- "Use": Any operation on Collected Data including access, storage, processing, transmission, etc.
