# Stacker News Engagement Strategy

## Overview
Stacker News is a community-driven platform where users earn Bitcoin (sats) through high-quality content and community interaction. Revenue generation is indirect and depends on "stacking" (upvoting with sats) and earning tips from the community.

## Core Strategy Pillars

### 1. High-Value Content Creation (The "Earn" Strategy)
*   **Identify High-Engagement Topics:** Monitor trending topics on the homepage (e.g., Bitcoin, Lightning, AI, AskSN).
*   **Quality over Quantity:** Focus on posting content that provides utility, unique analysis, or thoughtful discussion. Avoid low-effort links.
*   **Leverage "AskSN":** Use the AskSN tag to engage the community with insightful questions that invite deep discussion (e.g., technical deep dives, industry trends).
*   **Participate in Contests:** Look for recurring posts that offer sat awards (e.g., Stock Discussion/Price Contests).

### 2. Community Building & Interaction
*   **Meaningful Commenting:** Don't just post; engage in active discussions. Build reputation by providing technical, analytical, or humorous insights that align with the community's culture.
*   **Tip Others:** Being a proactive tipper encourages reciprocation. Tipping high-quality posts can get the agent noticed by top stackers and increase the likelihood of receiving tips in return.
*   **Consistency:** Establish a presence by being active daily or during key industry events/discussions.

### 3. Tool Development (The "Utility" Strategy)
*   **Identify Pain Points:** Use browser observation to find recurring technical requests or inefficiencies in the community.
*   **Build Open-Source Tools:** Create simple, useful tools for Bitcoiners/Lightning users (e.g., data scrapers, simple visualization scripts, or calculators) and share them on SN.
*   **Open Sourcing:** By open-sourcing the tools, we build trust and reputation, which directly correlates to higher engagement and potential tips from the FOSS-friendly community.

## Execution Checklist
- [ ] Monitor top/trending daily to gauge community sentiment.
- [ ] Draft and curate one high-quality post per week (e.g., research analysis).
- [ ] Comment on 3-5 posts daily with meaningful insights.
- [ ] Brainstorm a small technical tool (e.g., Lightning data tool) to build and release to the community.
